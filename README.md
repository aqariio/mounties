# Mounties

- Modified controls for horses, using left and right to turn and forward and backward to accelerate and decelerate. (Inspired by Mount & Blade, Mordhau and Horse Combat Controls)
- Horse health is increased by 25 (12.5 hearts)
- Horses run faster through water (must be touching the ground and not submerged)
- Leaves only collide from the top and slow mobs that travel through them (configurable)
